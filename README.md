## Free for everyone

- Type any song and get **karaoke versions with the words on screen**
- **Sing full screen** on a phone, a tablet, a computer or a TV browser
- If a version can't play, **the next one starts by itself**
- Your recent songs, kept on your device

## Party Pack — for karaoke nights

- **A singer queue** with a name for each song, played one after another
- **Fair turns, duets for two**, and a countdown that calls the next singer
- **Playlists saved** with your account, on up to 3 devices

Party Pack is free for 3 days, then **€1.99 a month**, **€19.99 a year** (two months free) or **€2.99 a month for two people**. Paid with PayPal, cancel anytime.
